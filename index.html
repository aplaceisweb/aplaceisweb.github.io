Lesson 7: a sunflower in 16 × 16 pixels

Pixel Garden · lesson 7 of 12 · posted one pixel at a time

A sunflower is the perfect second drawing. It has a clear shape, only a few colors, and it forgives you: if a petal comes out crooked, it just looks like the wind.

  1. Outline first. One dark pixel at a time. Diagonals of one step look smooth; steps of two look jagged.
  2. Fill flat. Petals yellow, center brown, stem green. No shading yet.
  3. One shadow tone. Pick a darker yellow and put it under every petal, on the side away from the light.
  4. One highlight. A single bright pixel on the center makes it round. One is enough.
  5. Step back. Zoom out. If it still reads as a sunflower at a glance, you are done.

A pixel has no size of its own. It waits for a screen to give it one.
